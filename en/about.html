About us

Tortillas La Catracha is a family-owned business with over 10 years of experience in the food industry, dedicated to the daily manufacturing and distribution of corn tortillas crafted with original recipes to offer a unique final product with traditional flavor.

La Catracha gives its customers great options when shopping. All products are prepared and distributed daily—fresh and hot—to retail locations, providing consumers with unmatched quality and freshness for the American market.

Our factory's operational method is built on freshness and quality to achieve a final product with no equal in the market.
